You are looking at photographs of one room in a house. **Nothing in this room carries a label we could read, so there is no list to match against.** Say what things are here.

**This is the harder question and it is being asked because there is no alternative.** Where a room has nameplates, they are read first and you are handed the answers. Here there are none — so every answer below is recognised from shape and context, and every one is recorded as a guess.

## What counts as a thing

A durable item that lives in this house and would still be here next year: a water heater, an electrical panel, a range, a sump pump, a deck, a window well, a shutoff valve, a smoke alarm.

**Not:** furniture, decoration, food, tools left on a bench, people, pets, or anything a household would take with them when they move.

One photograph often holds several things; one thing is often in several photographs. Both are ordinary.

## Say less rather than more, and here is the measured reason

On a room where this question was asked without a list, eight things came back confidently wrong. **A sediment filter cartridge was reported as a reverse-osmosis system. A chemical metering pump was reported as a well pump. A captive-air pressure tank was reported as a propane tank. Two vessels were reported as four.**

**Every one of those was a plausible answer to *what is in this room*.** None was a lie; each was a shape that resembled something.

⚑ **So the failure mode to avoid is not carelessness. It is confidence at the boundary of what a photograph can carry.** A grey cylinder in a mechanical room is a grey cylinder. **If you cannot tell which kind, say so in `unsure` and leave it out.**

## What to return for each thing, in `additional`

- **`label`** — what a homeowner would call it, in a few words. **Include a make or model only if you can read it in a photograph.** A general label that is right is worth more than a specific one that is guessed.
- **`classId`** — an id from the supplied class list, copied exactly, **or `null`.** *If nothing fits, `null` and describe it in the label.* **That is a correct and expected answer** — the list is incomplete, and a thing it has never met is still a real thing in a real room. **A wrong class is believed; a missing one is chased.**
- **`mediaIds`** — every photograph showing it. At least one.
- **`model`** — the model or part number printed on **this object**, exactly as it reads, or `null`.
  ⚑ **Only what is printed on the thing you are naming.** A mechanical room holds
  several plates in one frame, and a number read off the unit behind this one is
  worse than no number: it will be believed. **If you cannot read a model number
  on this object, `null`.**
- **`whatYouCanSee`** — one clause naming what in the picture made you say this. *"Vertical white cylinder with a gas control valve and a flue at the top."* **A person reads this beside the photograph, so write it for them.**
- **`whatMakesItDifferent`** — one clause distinguishing it from the other things you are reporting in this room. ⚑ **This is the duplicate guard.** *If two entries would carry the same sentence, they are probably one object seen twice.*
- **`partOf`** — **optional.** If this is plainly a component of another item in this answer, name that item exactly as you labelled it. **Leave it out unless it is obvious.**

## Also return

- **`unsure`** — things you think may be present but would not stand behind, one line each, in plain words. ⚑ **This is not a lesser version of the answer; it is a different one**, and it is the right home for anything you would otherwise be tempted to round up.
- **`roomNote`** — one or two sentences on what this room appears to be for, where the photographs support it. Empty rather than narrated.

## Two fields you will not need

**`located` and `couldNotLocate` are empty here.** They exist because the same answer shape is used when there *is* a known list. **There is not one for this room, so leave both empty.**

## Returning nothing is a valid answer

A hallway of closed doors contains no things of this kind. **An empty list with a `roomNote` saying why is a correct result and not a failure.** Do not fill it.
